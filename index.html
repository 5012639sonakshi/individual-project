Catch fraud. Label less.

Banks can’t afford to check every transaction by hand. This demo shows how a computer can pick only the most useful, cheapest transactions to check and still catch fraud well.

How it works

1
Learn from a few labeled examples
2
Spot the cases it is confused about
3
Pick the cheap, useful ones
4
Ask the investigator for labels
5
Learn again and repeat

The demo simulates 12,000 fake transactions (about 5% fraud, each with a checking cost of 1 to 6 units), trains a Logistic Regression model, and compares three ways of choosing what to check, with a budget of 1,200 units, averaged over 5 runs.

Results come from simulated data and are not proof of performance on real bank data. Project by Sonakshi (25BTRCM002), guided by Dr. B. Narmada, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University).
